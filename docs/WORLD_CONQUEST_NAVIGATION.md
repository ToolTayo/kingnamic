# World Conquest navigation acceptance

Date: 12 October 2026, local Windows Chromium. Branch: `codex/world-conquest-navigation`, based on release `ebea9285618c2972fa756da727e9e6e0309f4fee`. Main and production were not changed.

## Reproduced problem

The release's fresh Heartmere Marches panel listed only Pinewatch, Greybank and Saintless Fen. These are local resource claims, not the five rival kingdoms. The frontier panel mostly referred players to Empire, whose siege controls appeared after commander proximity/report conditions. The southern-road label was also hidden until commander appointment. The kingdoms were implemented; their existence and discovery routes were poorly explained.

## Implementation

- World replaces the Marches tab label while retaining its existing ID and every resource claim. Five named objectives, a local-resource shortcut, current-region breadcrumb and an Empire shortcut make the two systems distinct.
- A compact terrain atlas is generated from the actual 60×48 March map. Numbered clue/keep markers match minimap numbers; a commander marker shows actual location. Unknown kingdoms retain clues and conceal strength. Finding the village trail does not prematurely locate Mossgate.
- Status, finite defender/reserve counts and capture blockers read the existing campaign ledger. No new quest ledger, rewards, discoveries or campaign mutations are generated by the interface.
- View waypoint focuses the real map. Move company submits ordinary validated orders; it does not teleport, advance time, or start a siege artificially. The commander reaches the scouting tile while escorts retain existing formations. Resting-escort selection excludes ordered guards and custom squads. Rejected orders retain their warning.
- Each route leg requires an explicit order. Tight arrival precision avoids cutting tactical corners. Cached trails refresh when collision topology or commander identity changes. Captured-hall visits target open ground beside the building.
- Initial route review found that the Redwater detour inadvertently activated Stonewake. Its revised southern heath screen avoids the quarry. Stonewake now has a report stop outside its activation radius and a separate southwestern approach. A repeatable recruited-company browser sample obtained the report and approached at 145 HP, versus 40.1 HP on the earlier northern route. This is route geometry, not weaker enemies or protection from combat.
- Existing finite zombie packs, patrols, warning timers, sieges, infection, recruitment, garrisons and the global 200-soldier cap remain unchanged.

- TypeScript and production build passed. **296 unit tests / 19 files** passed with the existing 30-second test timeout and two workers. An earlier concurrent browser/default-worker run timed out one balance case; the isolated complete rerun passed without changing tests or thresholds.
- Complete Empire run: **41/42 passed**, including all five natural sieges, fresh settlement journey, controls, infection, recruitment, mobile construction, 10/50/100/200-soldier stress and 200-ID region transitions. The failed profiling case had **zero UI-hook samples**, not zero rendered frames. Vite's timestamped module URL produced a second class on unversioned instrumentation import; the profiler now imports the live resource URL and retains its sample-validity floor.
- After final waypoint/error-feedback corrections, **all 8 World Conquest browser cases passed again**: genuine fresh-road/village journey, 320px and 390px touch planning, and five physically walked discovery/approach journeys with legal recruits. Stonewake and Redwater each kept the neighboring kingdom inactive. Rejected-order fault injection verifies error feedback without editing combat outcomes.
- The full natural campaigns use prepared mature saves and normal recruitment/combat, not a claim of five conquests earned from one fresh campaign. All five verified discovery, siege, capture, occupation and revisit/reload. Mossgate's 100 original IDs: 82 living + 18 fallen; Tallowmere's 181 tracked participants: 157 living + 24 fallen. Both ledgers had zero missing/duplicate IDs.
- Save schema and keys remain v2. Route presentation is transient; persistent discoveries, troop state, jobs, settlements and conquests use the existing decoder and ledger.
- **All 11 production offline cases passed**: World Conquest without campaign mutation, editing/rotation and once-paid rewards, connected settlements, plague/remains/squads, disconnected reopening, cached-build update, expedition extraction, exact recruitment to 200, legacy census migration/recruitment, fresh install/update/returning player and 320px/390px touch layouts. Real player browser storage was never used.
- Final production output: **1,556,213 bytes raw / 445,649 bytes gzip total**, including notices and worker. JS is 431,803 bytes gzip; CSS 11,951. The existing Phaser chunk-size advisory remains.

The active frontier atlas costs more UI work than Army, while these samples retain the same p95 frame interval. The first frontier 250 ms gap includes a 249.7 ms UI wrapper and nested simulation work. Its approximately aligned CPU window mixes UI reconciliation, rendering, idle/program samples and combat; it does not establish a single exclusive cause. The released baseline also records substantial outliers. In two repeats the UI maximum fell to 30.1 and 15.6 ms. The second run's 249.9 ms RAF gap did not correspond to a recorded main-thread long task of that duration. All seven profiling cases passed the unchanged sampling-validity assertions; no speculative engine changes were made. Occasional large gaps remain a monitoring priority.

Post-GC heap usage across the first five samples was 12.9–14.4 MB, versus 11.3–13.7 MB at the beginning. This bounded, short capture is not a leak certification. Nested subsystem costs must not be added. Single-run differences and slightly different final combat outcomes are not evidence of a deterministic gameplay change or statistically proven performance improvement.

For equivalent 45-second 200-soldier/120-infected profiles, use `KINGNAMIC_HARDWARE_PROFILE=1`, `KINGNAMIC_PROFILE_CONTROLLED=1`, and `KINGNAMIC_PROFILE_WORLD=1` for the active atlas/route sample. The controlled camera is (21,12) in both views. Baseline comparison serves a Git archive of `ebea928` on a separate local port; it does not switch or modify main. Subsystem wrappers include nested costs and are not additive.

## Limits and decision

These are automated browser playthroughs plus screenshot review, not new human feedback. Physical Android/iOS, Safari and a continuous human five-kingdom campaign were not tested. Large-battle outliers remain: the sustained 100-soldier sample recorded a 133 ms frame; the 200-soldier sample recorded 83.3 ms frame gaps and 95–99 ms long tasks. Typical p95 frames in those runs were 16.7 ms. No unrelated performance rewrite was attempted.

Waypoints are guidance, not safe zones or an automatic campaign. Existing hostile kingdoms and terrain can require detours after development or combat; players retain Army controls and retreat options. Previously explored adjacent kingdoms can still react to deliberate movement under the established proximity rules.

**GO for integration review:** the navigation change has passed its final focused browser, complete unit/build and production offline checks, with every existing natural kingdom campaign verified. The complete Empire run was not repeated as one green 42-case run after the instrumentation fix; its 41 passing cases and the separate final navigation/profile reruns are reported explicitly above. Physical-device performance and long-frame outliers remain risks, not certified outcomes.
